Privacy Policy

coil — kinetic speed reader · Effective September 3, 2026

coil is designed to work locally on your device. We do not operate any servers, and we do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

  • Your reading content — books you fetch, documents you import (PDF/EPUB/TXT), and saved news articles are stored only in your device's local browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB).
  • Your settings and progress — reading positions, bookmarks, speed, and theme preferences are stored locally.
  • Anything you write or keep — notes you add in the margin, passages you keep, words you are learning, and your record of nights read stay in local storage on your device. They are never uploaded, backed up by us, or readable by anyone else.

Deleting a book in the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app removes this information. We never see it.

Network requests the app makes on your behalf

coil only connects to the internet when a feature you use requires it. The services below receive your IP address and the specific request, as any web server does. We do not send them anything else, and we receive nothing back about you.

  • Project Gutenberg — when you browse, search, or fetch a public-domain book. This may contact gutenberg.org, its mirrors aleph.gutenberg.org and gutenberg.pglaf.org, and the gutendex.com catalogue index used for search and browsing.
  • RSS/Atom feeds you add — when you load a news feed, the app contacts the feed's own server.
  • Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) — when you double-click a word to look up its definition. Offline lookups use a bundled dictionary and make no request.
  • Public relay proxies (corsproxy.io, api.codetabs.com, api.allorigins.win) — the web (browser) version only, to work around browser cross-origin limits when fetching books or feeds. These relays see the URL being fetched. The Android and iOS apps connect directly and do not use them.
  • MyMemory Translation API (mymemory.translated.net) — when you use in-app translation on a device without Chrome's on-device Translator API (Android, iOS, Safari, Firefox). The text you choose to translate is sent to this third-party service to be translated. On desktop Chrome, translation instead runs entirely on-device and nothing is sent anywhere.

The app's typefaces are bundled with the app itself — no font service is contacted.

Purchases

The optional Premium unlock is processed entirely by the store you installed the app from — Google Play Billing on Android, or Apple's App Store (StoreKit) on iPhone and iPad. We receive only an anonymous confirmation that the purchase succeeded — never your name, email, or payment details.

Sharing a passage

When you choose to share a passage, coil renders an image on your device — the passage, over the background you were reading it under — and hands it to your device's own share sheet, together with a link to the app's page. Where that image goes is entirely your choice at that moment; it is never uploaded to us, and we have no record that you shared anything. If you save the image instead, it goes only to your device.

Location

coil never requests or uses your location. The backgrounds that follow the time of day or the phase of the moon are computed from your device's own clock alone.

Read-aloud and Kids Mode

The "Read to Me" feature uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. Depending on your device settings, your operating system's speech service may process the spoken text. Kids Mode collects no information whatsoever and shows no advertising.

Children's privacy

coil does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. There is nothing to collect: the app has no accounts, no messaging, and no data transmission beyond the content requests listed above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of coil changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised.
